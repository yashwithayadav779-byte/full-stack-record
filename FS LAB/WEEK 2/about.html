About the Training & Placement Department

Anil Neerukonda Institue of Technology & Sciences (ANITS) has a dedicated Training & Placement Department led by Prof.P.Padmajaand supported by 18 Faculty Coordinators.The Department is committed to maximising student placenmets in both software and core engineering sectors through continuous skill development and industry engagement

ANITS provides comprhensive Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) covering Quantitative Aptitude,Logical Reasoning,Verbal Reasoning,English Communication,Group Discussions, and Interview Skills. These programs are conducted by experienced in-house trainers to prepare the students for campus recruitment.

Recruiter feedback and alumni performance are regularly analysed to improve training strategies and align them with industry requirements. The institute offers excellent placement infrastructure, including a 400-seat air conditioned auditorium,computer laboratories and examination halls accomodating over 500 students , and well-equipped interview rooms for smooth recruitment processes.

Through dedicated efforts, continuous improvement, and strong collaboration with industry, ANITS has consistently achieved an excellent placement record. The institute is recognized as one of the Top Ten Most Preferred Private Engineering Colleges in Andhra Pradesh by APSCHE among more than 700 engineering institutions.

Our Department Focuses On

  • Identifying employment opportunities for fresh engineering graduates.
  • Building strong industry–academia partnerships.
  • Providing comprehensive campus recruitment training programs.
  • Organizing industry expert lectures, workshops, and interactive sessions.
  • Promoting innovation and entrepreneurship development.
  • Facilitating internships, industrial visits, and technical collaborations.
